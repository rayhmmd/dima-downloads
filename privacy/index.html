Dima

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Dima has no Dima-operated account, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or cloud service. The Dima developer does not receive your name, profile photo, prayer history, settings, location, or crash reports through the app.

Some features use operating-system or third-party network services as described below. Prayer-time and Qibla calculations themselves run on your device.

Location and city lookup

  • iPhone and Mac: If you grant location access, Dima reads your device's coordinates for prayer times and Qibla. Dima may send coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to obtain a city and country name. Searching for a city uses Apple's MapKit search service, so Apple receives the search request under its own terms.
  • Windows: Dima does not request device location permission. Automatic location uses ipapi.co, with ipwho.is as a fallback. These providers receive the network request and its IP address and return an approximate location. Manual city search sends the search text and app language to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. Those providers handle the request under their own terms.

Dima stores the last accepted location or manually selected city locally so it can restore the same prayer schedule after relaunch. Choosing another location replaces that saved selection.

Data stored locally

  • Your first name, profile photo, settings, onboarding state, and prayer history are stored in Dima's local app storage.
  • Notification preferences and delivery state are stored locally, and reminders are scheduled or delivered by the operating system.
  • On iPhone and Mac, pairing credentials are stored in the system Keychain.
  • On iPhone and Mac, Dima may save a limited local report for an uncaught app exception. Reports omit the exception reason and user-info payload, are readable only by the local user/app identity, and are limited to the ten newest reports.

Dima does not automatically upload local data or diagnostic reports.

Paired prayer-history sync

If you explicitly pair Dima on an iPhone and Mac, the two devices exchange the full prayer-completion history directly over the local network. The exchange is encrypted using the pairing key. It does not pass through a Dima-operated server. Disconnecting prevents future sync but does not erase history already stored on either device.

Copying and sharing diagnostics

On supported Apple builds, you can review, delete, copy, or use the system share sheet for a local exception report. Copying or sharing is a user-initiated action; the selected destination then handles that report under its own terms.

Your control and deletion

You can revoke Apple location, camera, notification, and local-network permissions in system settings. Dima's in-app controls let you change location and notification preferences and disconnect a paired device.

Uninstall behavior differs by platform and distribution method. Removing the app may leave preferences, application-support files, Windows per-user data, or Keychain items on the device. Do not rely on uninstall alone as a guarantee that all local Dima data has been erased.

Data sale, advertising, and children

Dima does not sell or rent data and contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDK. Dima does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a Dima-operated service because it operates no such service.
